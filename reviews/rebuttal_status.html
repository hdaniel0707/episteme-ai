Findings and decisions from session 2

  • The paper's BaseJudge used the endpoint's default settings (Chat Completions, structured answer, no temperature/effort sent). The probe showed luna reasons adaptively under them (~4/12 calls at 2 decimals, ~10/12 at 4 decimals). The paper says "default settings", never "no reasoning".
  • Framing: LogJudge wins, and wins by more when BaseJudge is called like LogJudge. Do not present FusionJudge built from the matched BaseJudge (it is worse than LogJudge; not our proposition).
  • Repeated calls are presented as a stability result in the results section, not as a limitation. Per-text decision flips: only one calm sentence (1 of 400 random controls changed sides; 0 of 243 human controls crossed FusionJudge's threshold). No per-text std is presented.
  • The near-threshold selection did not bias A3b (controls never crossed; repeats came out at or below the paper). No all-human recapture needed.
  • Label wording: stability framing: order fine, ranking preserved, AT-HM can drop for other words because of saturation on rounded logprobs; human/machine keeps doubt.
  • Rerun every experiment that used the matched BaseJudge unintentionally (A1 precision, A4 averaging, gemma A1) with the paper's call; keep A2 and the A1 matched control matched on purpose.
  • Open question for co-authors: was w=½ for FusionJudge fixed a priori? (needed for Lma8 Q1 / C2)
