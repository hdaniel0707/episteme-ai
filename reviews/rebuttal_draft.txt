Thank you for the concrete requests. We address each weakness below.

**W1 (residual mass).** [READY]
New App. X reports label-absence rates, residual-mass distributions near the
threshold, and TPR@1%FPR for k = 1-20 under four residual treatments. Near the
threshold the residual is small (< 0.5%) but it is what orders the texts: rules that
keep it (ours, r -> human) give the same result, rules that discard it fail.
TPR@1%FPR, four-split mean, gpt-5.6-luna:

| treatment of r | k = 1 | k = 20 |
|---|---|---|
| r -> 1/2 (ours) | 0.648 | 0.648 |
| renormalize | 0.000 | 0.000 |

Our rule is insensitive to k (gemma-4-31b: 0.509 vs 0.512).
